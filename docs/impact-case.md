# WAYPOINT — Business & Customer Impact Case

> **FEG Innovation Hackathon 2026**  
> **Challenge 1 — Session Quality and Session-to-Action Conversion**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

In high-stakes, time-critical sports betting environments, high-intent customers frequently abandon sessions due to unexpected technical friction: sudden network drops during wager submission, live odds drift shock, or accidental tab closures. Historically, operators have treated these drop-offs as inevitable churn or diagnosed them hours later through post-hoc analytics.

**Waypoint** transforms silent drop-offs into visible, protected, and resilient user sessions. By introducing client-authoritative Session Intelligence, the **Session Lifeboat**, and **Continue Playing** re-entry, Waypoint preserves customer intent, eliminates duplicate transaction risks, and creates social engagement through **Live Match Rooms**.

## 3. Customer Impact

1. **Zero Uncertainty on Bet Placement**:
   When network drops occur mid-placement, traditional sportsbooks leave the user in limbo: *Did the bet place? Will I be charged twice if I tap again?* Waypoint's **Session Lifeboat** explicitly guarantees transaction safety, showing clear status and safely reconciling tickets upon reconnection.
2. **Effortless Continuity (Continue Playing)**:
   Customers who accidentally close the browser or lose signal return directly to their active fixture context, restored betslip selections, and match room interactions.
3. **Community & Non-Monetary Social Fun**:
   Event-anchored **Live Match Rooms** allow friends to track games, share non-monetary score predictions, and climb community leaderboards without requiring high wagering volumes.

---

## 4. Operator & Commercial Impact

1. **Turnover Protection Without Aggression**:
   Rather than pushing aggressive reload bonuses or urgency timers that run afoul of regulators, Waypoint protects naturally occurring customer demand by removing technical friction.
2. **Real-Time Funnel Diagnostics**:
   Operators gain direct insight into where players encounter cognitive thrash, odds drift shocks, or latency spikes through the **Universal Funnel** and **Friction Root Cause Chain**.
3. **Regulatory Goodwill & Responsible Gaming**:
   Waypoint's Responsible Intelligence engine strictly blocks manipulative mechanics (countdown pressure, frequency nudging, loss-chasing cues), positioning the operator as an industry leader in safe, respectful entertainment.
