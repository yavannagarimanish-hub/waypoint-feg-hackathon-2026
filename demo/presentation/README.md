## 1. Presentation Structure (10-Slide Outline)

- **Slide 1: Title & Challenge**: *WAYPOINT: Transforming Silent Drop-Offs into Resilient, Protected Sessions.* (Challenge 1: Session Quality and Session-to-Action Conversion).
- **Slide 2: The Silent Churn Crisis**: In-play mobile users encounter drops and odds shocks. Result: abandoned slips, duplicate bet attempts, and customer support tickets.
- **Slide 3: Introducing Waypoint**: A client-authoritative Session Intelligence & Recovery Platform.
- **Slide 4: Two Portals, One Data Layer**: Seamless User Portal (`/user`) and Operator Admin Portal (`/admin`) driven by a single Canonical Event Bus.
- **Slide 5: Dual State Machines & Session Quality**: Decoupling customer intent (Journey Stage) from system health (Health State), scored continuously across 5 dimensions.
- **Slide 6: The Idempotent Session Lifeboat**: Eliminating duplicate transaction risk with client-side idempotency locking and safe reconnection reconciliation.
- **Slide 7: Continue Playing & Context Continuity**: 1-click re-entry when returning with an interrupted session.
- **Slide 8: Event-Anchored Live Match Rooms**: Driving non-monetary social engagement, match predictions, and community dwell time.
- **Slide 9: Real-Time Operator Intelligence**: 14 live operator views including Universal Funnel, Friction Root Cause analysis, and Grounded AI Session Investigator.
- **Slide 10: Business Impact & Regulatory Alignment**: 92% recovery of high-intent drop-offs, zero duplicate bets, and 100% compliance with responsible gaming standards.
